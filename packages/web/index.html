Science On a Sphere — installation simulator

A projected sphere, and the calibration that fixes it. The picture is drawn by a shader; every number beside it is computed by the project's own forward model, and the two are compared against each other continuously — that disagreement is printed, not assumed. Press Recalibrate and the inverse model runs a full structured-light solve in your browser.
